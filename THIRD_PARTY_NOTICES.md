# Third-party components and research

- ECharts, Apache-2.0: native chart components, distributed through npm.
- Vite, MIT: development server and static build.
- SGLang, Apache-2.0: launched from user-provided engine images; native benchmark CLI is called without modification.
- GitHub Actions runner and official checkout/setup-node/artifact actions are used through their standard interfaces.

Design research referenced SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX (Apache-2.0) and SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX-app (GPL-3.0). This project does not copy their code, assets or branding. It is not an official InferenceX instance.

Internal Ku documents informed measurement caveats. Their bodies, credentials, signed links and attachments are not redistributed here.
